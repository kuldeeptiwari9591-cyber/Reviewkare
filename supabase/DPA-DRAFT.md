## Scope and instructions

The operator processes workspace data needed to host the workspace, authenticate owners, manage QR and review workflows, deliver account notices, and support the customer. The parties must document which processing the customer instructs the operator to perform and which activities each party handles for its own purposes. Workspace data may include account contact details, business profile settings, entered review text, replies, and usage records. Payment credentials are handled by the configured payment provider. The customer will use only data it is authorized to provide and follow the service's restrictions on sensitive and child data.

## Safeguards and subprocessors

The operator will restrict access to authorized staff with a need to know, require confidentiality, use the documented authentication and encryption controls, and review access periodically. List each authorized subprocessor, its function, region, data categories, and applicable contract in [schedule]. Agree on notice and objection procedures for changes and any conditions on cross-border processing before enabling a new provider.

## Requests and incidents

The operator will assist the customer with authorized access, correction, export, and deletion requests using the agreed contact. If the operator confirms a relevant security incident, it will notify the customer using the agreed escalation contact, share available facts, and cooperate on investigation and remediation. The parties will fill in the contract notification deadline and escalation path after checking applicable legal requirements.

## Return, retention, and audit

Document the access, export, deletion, payment record, audit log, and backup rotation periods in [retention schedule]. At termination, return or delete processor-held workspace data according to that schedule, subject to documented legal retention. The parties will agree on appropriate evidence, audit access, confidentiality, and the scope of compliance assistance.
